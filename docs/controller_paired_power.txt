=== the win/loss gate, as a hypothesis test ===
  A "k of n paired wins" rule is a SIGN TEST. Under the null (the lever does
  nothing) each seed is a coin flip, so the gate fires by chance at:
  rule           P(fire|null) across 3 rungs
  4 of 4         0.0625       0.1760
  3 of 4         0.3125       0.6750
