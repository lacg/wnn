Timeline: Now through approximately June-July 2026 (notification dates TBD by RAID).

- [ ] **Talk to Axway manager/HR about professional development budget**
  - Ask specifically: "Is there a conference attendance / professional development budget I can tap if I get a peer-reviewed paper accepted at an academic security conference?"

    - Bringing back knowledge to the team
    - Would you (Axway) want me to acknowledge the company in the paper?

  - **Outcome to aim for**: soft commitment (verbal or email) that Axway will cover some/all of registration + travel

  - Employee handbook → search for "conference", "training", "professional development", "education reimbursement"

  - Some companies require approval upfront; others reimburse after

- [ ] **Set up passive monitoring**
  - Bookmark https://raid2026.org/registration.html (check monthly for published fees + travel grant announcements)
  - Subscribe to RAID mailing list if available
  - Follow @raid_conference / organisers on social media for updates

- [ ] **Build travel savings buffer**
  - Worst case self-fund: budget ~$4,000 USD
  - Start setting aside ~$500/month = $2,500 by September
  - Even a partial employer reimbursement cuts this in half

## Phase 2: UPON ACCEPTANCE NOTIFICATION (~June-July 2026)

The best time to act is immediately after receiving the acceptance email. You'll have 3-4 months before the conference.

### Immediate actions (within 1 week of notification)

- [ ] **Forward acceptance email to Axway manager**
  - Subject: "RAID 2026 paper accepted — requesting conference sponsorship"
  - Include: paper title, conference name, dates, Lancaster UK, link to conference website
  - Attach: PDF of acceptance email and your paper (anonymized version is fine)
  - Ask for: formal approval + budget code for registration + travel
  - Follow up in person within 3 days

- [ ] **Check RAID 2026 registration page for travel grants**
  - By notification time, fees and travel grant programs should be announced
  - If a grant program exists, note the application deadline and required materials

- [ ] **Email RAID PC chairs about financial hardship (if needed)**
  - Only if employer sponsorship is declined
  - Contact: `raid26.pc.chairs@gmail.com`
  - Polite, honest, brief:
    ```
    Subject: Financial Assistance Inquiry — Accepted Paper [Title]

    Dear RAID 2026 PC Chairs,

    My paper "[title]" has been accepted for RAID 2026 (thank you!).
    I am an independent researcher without institutional funding.
    Could you advise whether any travel assistance or fee reduction
    is available for authors in this situation?

    Regards,
    Luiz Alberto Crispiniano Garcia
    ```
  - Realistic expectations: conferences sometimes waive registration, rarely cover travel

- [ ] **Apply to ACM SIGSAC programs (if any announced for RAID 2026)**
  - ACM sometimes extends travel grants to non-CCS security conferences
  - Check https://www.sigsac.org for any open programs
  - Note: most SIGSAC programs require student status

### Within 2 weeks of notification

- [ ] **Book flights early for better prices**
  - Target: ~3-4 months before conference = best price/flexibility
  - Recommended routes from US East Coast to Lancaster:
    - EWR/JFK → MAN (Manchester) → Lancaster (1h train, ~£15)
    - Or EWR/JFK → LHR (London) → Lancaster (3h train, ~£80)
  - Use Google Flights alerts to track prices
  - Only book after employer sponsorship is confirmed (or if self-funding is decided)

- [ ] **Book hotel near the venue**
  - RAID 2026 venue: check conference site for recommended hotels or block-booked rates
  - Typical conference rates: £120-200/night for 4 nights
  - Target: walking distance to venue, 3-4 star business hotel

- [ ] **Check UK visa requirements**
  - As a Brazilian citizen: UK allows visa-free entry for short stays (tourism, business, conferences) up to 6 months
  - You'll need: valid Brazilian passport (minimum 6 months validity from travel date)
  - Bring: acceptance letter, conference details, return ticket, hotel booking — for border questions
  - If you have US permanent residence / green card, that may simplify entry
  - Double-check closer to travel date at https://www.gov.uk/check-uk-visa

  - Flip `\anonymoustrue` → `\anonymousfalse` in LaTeX

- [ ] **Register for the conference**
  - Use the Early Bird rate if registering before the deadline (usually ~1-2 months before)

### Presentation preparation

- [ ] **Prepare the talk** (20-30 min slide deck + backup slides for Q&A)
  - Core narrative: evolutionary WNN for IDS, three datasets, FPGA deployment
  - Headline number: 100 KB classifier on $20 FPGA, 5.8× lower FPR than RF on CIC-IoT
  - Practice the talk 5-10 times; target 18-22 minutes for a 25-min slot
  - Anticipate likely reviewer questions (see paper read-through notes)

- [ ] **Prepare backup materials**
  - Printed copies of the paper (5-10 for networking)
  - Business cards (even informal: name + ORCID + email)
  - Notebook for conversations / follow-up ideas
  - Power adapter for UK outlets (Type G, 3-pin)

### Travel logistics

- [ ] **Travel insurance**
  - Independent researcher → no institutional coverage
  - Basic travel + medical insurance for UK: ~$50-150 for 1 week
  - Check if your US health insurance provides any international coverage
  - Recommended: World Nomads, SafetyWing, or similar

- [ ] **Lancaster logistics**
  - Train tickets: book via https://www.thetrainline.com (cheaper if booked early)
  - Local transport: Lancaster is walkable; Uber/taxi available
  - Weather in October: 8-14°C, rainy → bring layers + waterproof

## Contingency Plans

### If Axway declines sponsorship
1. Apply to any announced RAID 2026 travel grants
2. Email PC chairs for financial assistance
3. Apply to ACM/IEEE general travel assistance programs
4. Self-fund with savings + consider the investment value (peer-reviewed publication, career credit)
5. Last resort: virtual presentation if allowed (some conferences permit this for financial hardship)

### If funding is partial
- Prioritize: registration fee (non-negotiable) > flight > hotel > meals
- Stay at budget hotel or Airbnb instead of conference hotel (save $200-400)
- Fly economy, book early, use frequent flyer miles if available
- Eat most meals at conference (usually included in registration)

### If visa/entry issues arise
- RAID 2026 organizers typically provide invitation letters for visa applications
- Contact `raid26.pc.chairs@gmail.com` early if visa support needed
- Virtual presentation as absolute fallback
